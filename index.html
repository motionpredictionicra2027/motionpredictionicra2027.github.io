Area of Interest

Why Long-term Motion Prediction?

Anticipating human motion is a key skill for intelligent systems that share a space or interact with humans. Accurate long-term predictions of human movement trajectories, body poses, actions or activities may significantly improve the ability of robots to plan ahead, anticipate the effects of their actions or to foresee hazardous situations.

The topic has received increasing attention in recent years across several scientific communities with a growing spectrum of applications in service robots, self-driving cars, collaborative manipulators or tracking and surveillance. This workshop is the ninth in a series of ICRA 2019–2027 events. Its aim is to bring together researchers and practitioners from different communities and to discuss recent developments in this field, promising approaches, their limitations, benchmarking techniques and open challenges.

Focus

Areas of Interest

Motion prediction connects many communities. This year we focus on the following application areas.

🧭

Social and Predictive Navigation

Exploring the intersection of human behavior and robot navigation: the latest advancements in algorithms and applications that enhance the safety and efficiency of robots in dynamic, human-inhabited environments.

🚗

Automated Driving

The critical role of motion prediction in autonomous vehicles: challenges of real-time decision-making and safety in self-driving technologies.

🦾

Collaborative and Production Robots

How motion prediction can improve human-robot collaboration in industrial and service settings, with anticipatory algorithms that enable robots to work alongside humans more safely and effectively.

🧠

Multi-modal Foundation Model Integration

Combining visual, sensory and contextual information, and leveraging foundation models, to enhance the predictive capabilities of robotic systems.

Call for Papers

We welcome researchers in the field to submit papers to be presented in pitch talks and as posters. Submissions will be reviewed and selected based on their originality, relevance to the workshop topics, contributions, technical clarity, and presentation.

4 pages Paper length

At most 4 pages, excluding references.

IEEE Format

Two-column format, using the paper template from the ICRA 2027 website.

Video Optional

We encourage authors to additionally submit a video clip to complement their manuscript.

Poster Presentation

Accepted papers are presented in pitch talks and in the poster session.

Topics of Interest

We invite original contributions related to long-term human motion prediction. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Motion trajectory prediction in 2D and 3D
  • Predicting articulated human motion
  • Early action and activity recognition
  • Motion and task planning in dynamic environments considering motion predictions
  • Uncertainties related to prediction inputs/outputs and their propagation
  • Anticipation of group and crowd motion
  • Human motion prediction and safety
  • Human-robot interaction considering predictions
  • Evaluation of prediction algorithms: datasets, metrics, and benchmarks
  • Predictive planning and control
  • Applications of motion prediction techniques
  • Visual scene prediction
